Privacy Policy

Preston Recruiting · Effective October 3, 2026

Who uses this app

Preston Recruiting is a personal tool used only by its owner, Preston Becker, with his own Google account. No one else's account is connected to it.

What Google data it accesses and why

  • Create email drafts (gmail.compose): to place recruiting emails in the owner's Drafts folder. The owner reviews and sends each one himself. The only email the app sends on its own is a weekly summary to the owner and his parent; it never emails coaches or anyone else.
  • Read mail (gmail.readonly): only to see whether a drafted email was sent and whether the coach it was sent to has replied, so follow-ups can be tracked.

What is stored

A tracking record in the owner's private GitHub repository: the coach's name, school and email address, the dates an email was drafted, sent and replied to, and the first line of a coach's reply. No message bodies, attachments or unrelated mail are stored.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any AI or machine-learning model. It is used only to support the owner's own recruiting correspondence.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Preston Recruiting's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, the tracking record will be deleted.
