Preston Recruiting

A personal tool for one student-athlete's college lacrosse recruiting.

Preston Recruiting is a private, single-user tool used by Preston Becker to organize his own outreach to college lacrosse coaches. It prepares email drafts in Preston's Gmail account for him to review and send himself, and keeps track of which coaches have replied.

It is not a public service. It does not offer accounts to other people, and it never sends email on its own.
